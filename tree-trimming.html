Tree Trimming in Lehi

Trimming does more than tidy things up — it removes weak or crossing branches before they break in a windstorm, opens up airflow so the canopy stays healthy, and keeps limbs away from your roof, siding, and gutters. We cut with the tree's natural shape in mind instead of just hacking back growth.

Timing matters too. Some species in Utah County are better pruned in late winter before new growth starts, while others can be trimmed almost any time of year. If you're not sure what your tree needs or when, tell us what you're seeing and we'll give you a straight answer on the call.

Frequently Asked Questions About Tree Trimming

How often should trees be trimmed?

Most established trees do well with trimming every 2-3 years. Fast-growing species, fruit trees, or trees near structures often need it yearly. We can suggest a schedule based on what's on your property.

Can you trim trees that are close to power lines?

Yes, we regularly trim branches that are approaching utility lines. For lines that are already in contact with a tree, we'll coordinate with the utility company before starting work.

Will trimming hurt my tree?

Done correctly, no — proper pruning cuts encourage healthy new growth. We avoid over-thinning or topping, which are the two things that actually stress a tree out.
