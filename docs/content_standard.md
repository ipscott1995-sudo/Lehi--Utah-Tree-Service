# Content Standard

## Audience

Write primarily for homeowners, apartment owners, and residential property owners.

The reader is usually trying to solve a practical problem quickly.

## Voice

The tone should be:

- Clear
- Local
- Professional
- Helpful
- Confident
- Slightly premium
- Not cheesy

## Avoid

Do not use phrases like:

- we understand the importance of
- look no further
- your trusted partner
- in today's fast-paced world
- we are passionate about
- top-rated
- #1 rated
- best in the area
- thousands of satisfied customers

Do not invent:

- reviews
- ratings
- founders
- years in business
- awards
- licenses
- certifications
- physical addresses
- Google Business Profile claims

## Reading Level

Aim for 8th-grade clarity.

Use short paragraphs.

Most paragraphs should be 2–4 sentences.

Avoid walls of text.

## SEO Writing

Use keywords naturally.

Do not keyword stuff.

Primary keyword format:

- [service] in [city], [state]
- [niche] in [city]
- emergency [service] near [city]

Use city and state naturally in:

- H1
- first paragraph
- title tag
- meta description
- one or two subheadings

## Service Page Copy Length

Target 900–1,400 words per service page.

## Homepage Copy Length

Target 1,200–1,800 words.

## Location Page Copy Length

Target 700–1,200 words.

## FAQ Count

FAQ page should include 15–25 questions.

Service pages should include 4–6 FAQs.

Location pages should include 4–6 FAQs.

## Human Editability

All AI copy should be easy to manually edit.

Avoid overly complex generated structures.

Prefer plain paragraphs, lists, and headings.
