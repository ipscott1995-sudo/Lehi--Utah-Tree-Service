Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We work throughout Lehi and nearby Utah County communities, including Saratoga Springs, Eagle Mountain, American Fork, Lindon, Pleasant Grove, Highland, and Alpine.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, estimates are free. Call (385) 534-4337 or fill out our quote form and we'll set up a time to take a look.

What are your hours?

We're available Monday through Friday 7am-7pm and Saturday 8am-5pm, with 24/7 emergency service for storm damage and fallen trees.

How quickly can you schedule a job?

It depends on the season and what the job involves, but most non-emergency work can be scheduled within a few days of your estimate.

Do you clean up after the job is done?

Yes, cleanup is included. We haul away branches, logs, and debris unless you'd rather we leave the wood for you.

How do I get a quote?

Call (385) 534-4337, email contact@lehitreepros.com, or fill out our online quote form and we'll follow up with next steps.
