Tree Removal for Lehi Homeowners

Not every tree is worth saving, and in Lehi's wind and winter snow load, a dead or weakened tree near a house, driveway, or fence is a real risk. We assess the lean, the root condition, and what's nearby before recommending removal — if trimming or cabling can fix the problem instead, we'll say so.

Tight lots are common in newer Lehi neighborhoods, so we plan each removal around what's actually around the tree: power lines, sheds, neighboring fences, gardens. Larger trees come down in sections when there isn't room for a straight drop. Stump removal is available as a separate service if you want the area fully cleared.

Frequently Asked Questions About Tree Removal

How do I know if a tree needs to come down or just needs trimming?

If more than a quarter of the tree is dead, the trunk has a deep lean or split, or the roots are visibly lifting near the base, removal is usually the safer call. We'll walk the tree with you and explain what we're seeing before recommending anything.

Will removal damage my lawn or driveway?

We plan the drop zone and use mats or boards under equipment on grass and pavers when needed. For tight spaces, we section the tree down by rope instead of dropping it whole.

Do you remove the stump too?

We can grind the stump as part of the same visit or schedule it separately — let us know what you'd like when you request your estimate.
