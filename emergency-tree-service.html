24/7 Emergency Tree Service in Lehi

Utah County storms can bring down limbs and entire trees with little warning, and a tree resting on a roof or blocking a driveway isn't something that can wait until Monday. Call (385) 534-4337 and we'll talk through what you're dealing with and get someone out as soon as we can.

Our first priority in an emergency is making the situation safe — securing or removing what's hazardous, clearing access to your home, and getting weight off a structure if a tree has fallen on it. From there we'll talk about full removal, cleanup, and whether you'll need anything for an insurance claim.

Frequently Asked Questions About Emergency Tree Service

How fast can you get to an emergency?

Response time depends on the time of day and how many calls are ahead of you, but emergency calls are prioritized over routine scheduling. Call (385) 534-4337 and we'll give you a realistic estimate when you call.

Will you work with my insurance company?

We can document the damage with photos and provide details about the work for your claim. Filing the claim itself is between you and your insurer.

What should I do while I'm waiting for you to arrive?

Stay away from the tree and any hanging or broken limbs, especially if power lines are involved. Keep people and pets clear of the area until we get there.
